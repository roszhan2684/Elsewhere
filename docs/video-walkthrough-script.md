## Recording tips

- Record each line as its own take and drop it on its start time. Leave the gaps silent, because the clicks carry them.
- Smile on the hook and slow down on the last line.
- Burn in captions, because many judges watch muted.
- Add a 2–3 second end card with the logo and `github.com/roszhan2684/Elsewhere`. The launch film’s last 3 seconds work for this.
